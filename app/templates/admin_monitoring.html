Capture integrity

Checking the visit collector…

Whether the honeypot is actually recording the research data it exists to record. The collector never blocks a response — it drops a visit rather than delay one, and swallows the error — so a silent capture failure looks exactly like a healthy stack. The corpus figures are read from the central log itself; Visits lost comes from the collector, which is the only thing that knows what never arrived.
