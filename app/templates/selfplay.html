Adversarial Self-Play

A learned crawler is trained (PPO-Lagrangian, a constrained MDP) to harvest honeypot content while evading the production detector. The evasions it discovers harden a robust detector that runs as a second opinion on Crawler Analytics — and the infrastructure it must buy to defeat each fingerprint layer becomes a measured cost-to-evade curve.
