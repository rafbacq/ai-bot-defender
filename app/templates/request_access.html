Request access

Duke WATCH is available to approved research institutions. Argus Lab reviews each request before an account can be created.

  1. Submit the external access-request form.
  2. Wait for Argus Lab to review the request and approve the institutional email. Submitting the form alone does not create an account.
  3. After approval, open the signup page and use that exact email and institution to create your account.
